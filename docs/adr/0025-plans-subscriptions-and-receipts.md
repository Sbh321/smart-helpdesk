# ADR-0025 Plans, subscriptions with receipt payments, platform admin accounts and self sign-up

**Status:** Accepted (2026-09-25). Supersedes the "billing and subscriptions" line of [scope.md](../00-project/scope.md) §Out of scope for the forms decided here; online payment stays out ([V1-PL-03](../../roadmap/09-v1-backlog.md)). Roadmap [Milestone 6](../../roadmap/14-platform-admin-and-billing.md).

## Context

The platform console lists workspaces and nothing else: provisioning exists in the API and on the command line (`tenants:create`) but not in the console, platform admins exist only through `platform:create-admin`, they cannot reset a password and cannot be disabled, and there is no notion of a plan beyond a `tenants.plan` string nobody reads. The owner wants to run the product as a service now, without an online payment provider: a free trial, a paid plan for a period, payment proven by a receipt the workspace uploads and a platform admin verifies, more admins with a full account flow, self sign-up from the website, and a dashboard of the platform's numbers.

Owner decisions (2026-09-25): the workspace owner uploads the receipt and a platform admin approves or rejects it (an admin may also record a payment); a lapsed workspace gets a grace period with warnings and then becomes read-only until paid; workspaces are created from the console **and** by public self sign-up; all platform admins are equal (Platform Super Admin).

## Decision

1. **Plans** are control-plane data (`plans`), editable in the console: a code, name, description, kind (`trial` or `paid`), price in minor units and currency (NPR by default), the length of one billing period in months (paid) or the trial length in days (trial), active flag and order. Seeded: *Free trial* (14 days, free) and *Standard* (NPR 2,500 per month). At most one active trial plan: it is what a new workspace starts on.
2. **Subscription**: one row per workspace (`subscriptions`, central, keyed by `tenant_id`): the plan and `ends_at`. The state is **derived, never stored**: `trialing` or `active` (trial or paid plan, before `ends_at`), `grace` (after `ends_at`, before `ends_at + grace days`), `expired` (after that). Grace days are a platform setting (7). One function (`SubscriptionState`) computes it for PHP and one SQL expression for lists and counts; both are tested against the same cases. Existing workspaces were backfilled on *Standard* for a year, so nothing already running is blocked.
3. **Payments** (`subscription_payments`, central, keyed by `tenant_id`): the plan, the number of periods paid, amount and currency, date paid, method (bank transfer, wallet, cash, other), reference, note, the receipt, and a review state `pending → approved | rejected` (rejection needs a reason). **Approving** extends the subscription by periods × the plan's months from the later of now and the current `ends_at`, switches the plan, and records the period on the payment; it runs once under a row lock. Owners are emailed on approval and rejection; admins on a new receipt.
4. **Receipts are media items of the workspace** (a system *Billing* folder, upload purpose `receipt`, images or PDF, `billing.manage`), linked to the payment through `mediables`, so they are validated and sniffed like every upload, can never be purged while the payment exists, open only for people with `billing.manage`, and show in the lightbox. The console opens them through `GET /platform-api/payments/{id}/receipt`, which signs the storage URL inside the workspace's context.
5. **Central tables with a tenant key.** `subscriptions` and `subscription_payments` carry `tenant_id` (foreign key, cascade) without row-level security, like `domains`: the console reads across workspaces, and the workspace endpoints (`GET /v1/billing`, `POST /v1/billing/payments`) filter by the resolved tenant explicitly, covered by isolation tests. They are not in change capture (`entity_changes` is an application table); their history is the audit log (`subscription.*`, `payment.*`) and the review timestamps.
6. **Read-only after grace.** On the tenant API, `EnsureSubscriptionWritable` (after membership) answers every write in an `expired` workspace with 403 `workspace_read_only`, except sign-out, the user's own preferences, billing, and the media upload of a receipt. Reads and exports keep working. The session (`GET /v1/me`) carries the subscription summary so the SPA shows a banner (trial ending, grace, read-only) and a Billing page (settings, `billing.manage`, owner and admin roles). Reminder emails go 7, 3 and 1 days before `ends_at`, when grace starts and when the workspace becomes read-only (daily job, each sent once per period). MVP-SHORTCUT: inbound email still creates tickets in a read-only workspace (V1-PL-19).
7. **Platform admin accounts.** Admins are invited by email (48-hour single-use link to the admin host, accept sets name and password), can reset a forgotten password (Laravel password broker `platform_users`, 60 minutes, same neutral answer for unknown addresses), change their name and password, and are deactivated rather than deleted; deactivation ends their sessions and passes on the next request. Nobody deactivates themselves, and the last active admin cannot be deactivated. Every action is audited with no tenant.
8. **Self sign-up.** A public form on the app host (`/signup`, linked from the website) asks for name, email, password, workspace name and address; the API stores a pending sign-up (password hashed) and emails a 24-hour verification link; following it provisions the workspace on the trial plan with the person as its active owner, and sends them to sign in. The address is checked live, reserved and existing addresses are refused, a honeypot field and throttles (per IP and per address) limit abuse, and a platform setting turns sign-up off. The console creates workspaces through the same `ProvisionTenant` with any plan.
9. **Dashboard.** `GET /platform-api/dashboard` returns workspaces by state, new workspaces per week (12 weeks, console and self sign-up), active users, tickets in the last 30 days, approved revenue per month (12 months, per currency), pending receipts and subscriptions ending in the next 14 days. Counts inside workspaces are taken workspace by workspace in their own context (row-level security stays forced) and cached for five minutes. MVP-SHORTCUT → V1-PL-20 (a platform read model).

## Consequences

- New vocabulary: **Plan**, **Subscription**, **Payment**, **Receipt**, **Grace period**, **Sign-up** ([terminology](../00-project/terminology.md)).
- The permission catalogue gains `billing.manage` (owner and admin roles).
- `tenants.plan` stays in the schema but is no longer shown or read; the subscription is the source.
- A workspace can be locked out of writes by the calendar; the grace period, the reminders and the banner make that visible well before it happens, and approving a receipt lifts it immediately.
- Public sign-up is a new unauthenticated write path; it is rate limited, verified by email and can be switched off.
- A workspace without a subscription row (made directly in the database, as the test factories do) is `none`: unmanaged and never limited. Every provisioned workspace has one, and existing workspaces were backfilled.

## Alternatives considered

- **An online provider (Stripe, Paddle, eSewa, Khalti).** Out of scope for now; the payment record keeps a method and reference so a provider can fill it later (V1-PL-03).
- **Subscription state stored and advanced by the scheduler.** Rejected: a late or failed job would leave a wrong state; derived state is right at any moment, and the job only sends reminders.
- **Receipts in a platform bucket outside the workspace.** Rejected: it would duplicate upload validation, and the workspace could not see its own receipts in its library.
- **Billing tables with row-level security.** Rejected: every console list and the dashboard would have to visit each workspace; explicit filtering on the two workspace endpoints is smaller and tested.
