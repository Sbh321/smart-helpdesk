# Datasets v1

Created 2026-09-21. Never edited or regenerated in place; a change creates `v2` ([evaluation-methodology.md](../../../docs/05-algorithms/evaluation-methodology.md) §2).

| File | Content | Made by | Seed |
|---|---|---|---|
| `workload/` | 8 agents (capacities 5–12, 2–3 skills), 6 categories, 500 tickets with exponential inter-arrival (mean 120 s) and handling times (mean 90 min); see `workload/README.md` | `php artisan experiment:generate-workload --seed=42` | 42 |
| `duplicates.json` | 300 labelled pairs: 100 duplicates (60 generated by dropping and swapping words, 40 hand-written), 200 non-duplicates (50 same category, 150 different categories) | `php artisan experiment:generate-duplicates --seed=42` | 42 |
| `duplicates-haystack.json` | 5 000 generated tickets for Recall@5 (one per line) | same command | 43 |
| `sources/duplicates-hand-written.json` | the 40 hand-written duplicate pairs merged into `duplicates.json` | hand-written | — |
| `priority-scenarios.json` | 12 tickets with the expected score and level (worked examples of priority-scoring.md, the three threshold boundaries, the age cap) | hand-written | — |
| `sla-scenarios.json` | 10 timelines (5 on 24×7, 5 on working hours Sunday–Friday 10:00–17:00 Asia/Kathmandu, one with a holiday) with expected states, warning and due times and notification counts | hand-written | — |

Generated ticket texts come from a small phrase bank per category (`TicketTextGenerator`), so tickets of one category share words; the hand-written pairs were written and labelled by one person. Both are threats to validity (methodology §5).
